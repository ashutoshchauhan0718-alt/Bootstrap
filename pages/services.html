Electrical Power Infrastructure

Engineering · Execution · Testing · Commissioning

KALAGNI Integrated Projects Private Limited provides electrical power infrastructure engineering and project delivery services across HT/LT networks, substations, cable infrastructure, electrical distribution and transmission systems.

Our EPC delivery model connects concept design, engineering, statutory approval coordination, procurement, installation, testing, commissioning, handover and lifecycle support, according to the contracted scope.

service-electrical.jpg

Powering infrastructure for a brighter tomorrow.

Our Core Capabilities

Reliable power infrastructure for industries, cities and communities

01

Concept Design & Custom Engineering

  • Load-flow studies and short-circuit analysis
  • Single-line diagrams and design documentation
  • Protection and relay coordination
  • Substation layouts and equipment planning
  • Earthing and lightning-protection design
  • Project-specific panel, cable and network design
02

HT & LT Electrical Systems

  • HT/LT electrical infrastructure
  • Transformers, switchgear and ring main units (RMUs)
  • HT/LT panels and distribution systems
  • Bus ducts and associated electrical infrastructure
  • Power distribution for industrial and commercial facilities
03

Substations: GIS & AIS

  • Engineering and coordination for gas-insulated and air-insulated substations
  • Transformer, switchgear, protection and control interfaces
  • Auxiliary systems and SCADA integration where specified
  • Installation, testing and commissioning
04

Underground Cable Networks

  • HT/LT cable routing and network planning
  • Trench and duct coordination
  • Cable laying, jointing and termination
  • Inspection and testing according to project specifications
05

Overhead Transmission Lines

  • Transmission-line engineering and project coordination
  • Tower and conductor-related design coordination
  • Foundation, erection and stringing activities
  • Line testing and commissioning support
06

Electrical Panels & Control Systems

  • PCC and MCC panels
  • APFC panels
  • PLC and SCADA panels and interfaces
  • Energy metering and automation
  • BMS integration where specified

Our Approach

Compliance First

Plan work around applicable statutory requirements, approved specifications and relevant technical standards.

Design-Led Solutions

Develop project-specific engineering solutions based on requirements, site conditions and coordinated design.

Single-Point EPC Responsibility

Provide a coordinated point of responsibility for the work packages included in the contract.

Lifecycle Support

Support preventive maintenance, breakdown response and asset documentation where contracted.

Statutory Approvals Coordination

Depending on project requirements, location and applicable regulations, KALAGNI can coordinate documentation and interfaces with relevant authorities and utilities, such as:

  • CEIG or the applicable Electrical Inspectorate
  • DISCOMs and transmission utilities
  • PESO, where applicable to the project
  • Other relevant statutory bodies and approval authorities

Approvals remain subject to the competent authority's requirements and decisions. The exact authority names, jurisdiction and applicability are confirmed for each project.

Our Value Proposition

KALAGNI aims to provide customised, reliable and future-ready power infrastructure through engineering-led planning, coordinated execution and responsible project delivery.
